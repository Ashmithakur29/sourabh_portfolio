Selfie Stitch: Dual Homography Based Image Stitching for Wide‑Angle Selfie

International Conference on Multimedia and Expo (ICME) · Jul 27, 2018
Show publication

Abstract

This paper proposes an image stitching method for increasing the field of view of widely used face photographs called ‘selfies’. Unlike traditional methods that assume a single dominant plane, we focus on near‑field face geometry and model two dominant planes. Significant depth difference between face and background introduces parallax; to mitigate this, the image is segmented into foreground (face) and background, and two homographies are estimated independently. A per‑pixel homography is then obtained by weighting the foreground/background homographies using distance from the segmentation boundary. The approach produces aesthetically stitched face photographs with reasonable post‑processing time on mobile devices.
